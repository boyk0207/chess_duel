VoxelMeadow - 36 voxel models

Scale: 1 voxel = 10 cm, so a 1.8 m figure is 18 voxels tall. Props pivot at their bottom centre; modular pieces
(SM_Mod_*, SM_Ruin_*, SM_Kit_*) pivot at a bottom corner and snap on a 2 m grid.
Every model uses one palette texture: Textures/T_VoxelPalette.png for colour and Textures/T_VoxelPalette_E.png as
the glow mask (no model in this pack uses a glowing colour). Use nearest / point filtering and no mipmaps so colours stay clean; edit
the palette to recolour the whole pack. Faces are merged (no hidden faces), flat normals.
glTF 2.0 binary for Unity, Godot, three.js and others: Y up, metres, palette embedded.
