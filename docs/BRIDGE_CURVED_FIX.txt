Floating Island V4.2 — curved walkable suspension bridge
Restores the hanging U-shaped deck on all four bridges. 17 original wooden planks, original UV/texture and separate ropes/posts preserved. The two end planks are level at island Y=0, the other 15 form a smooth piecewise-linear sag of about 0.42 m; max grade angle 28.3 degrees. Each plank has its own matching sloped convex collider, with only 2 mm overlap at seams. Floor snap length is 0.32 m on both fighters to help them follow the slope; upward jumps remain possible. Damaged planks still lose their individual collisions and fall. No hidden continuous floor. Collapse movement unchanged.
Test with F5 or scenes/tests/animation_lab.tscn F6: walk, sprint in both directions over intact bridges before collapse; then test damaged bridge and ring-out. Godot runtime not available, so only geometry and archive checks were performed.
