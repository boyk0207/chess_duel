CHESS DUEL — FLOATING ISLAND V2
Base: chessduel_floating_graphics_update.zip

F5 runs only main.tscn. F6 on scenes/tests/animation_lab.tscn opens the duel test; F6 on scenes/tests/rig_preview.tscn opens the clip viewer. No test entry added to the main menu.

FEATURES
- Original textured rope bridge restored. Bridge_Segmented.glb has all original 478 triangles with original UVs and texture, split into 17 planks, 10 rope halves and 4 posts. Planks have individual approximate box colliders. Bridge attacks from specials can drop six adjacent interior planks once per bridge. Rope halves and remaining planks drop separately when the connected island collapses.
- Satellite islands and edge strips: five seconds of warning, glowing crack, increasing shake and dust; final 1.4 seconds tilt; then 1.8-second accelerating fall. The island's moving collision follows the visible island. Loose plank collisions are disabled as each plank starts falling.
- Four updrafts (cyan rings), reachable by jumping off the main/outer island edges. They lift both fighters; they turn off when the corresponding satellite drops. Moving east-side ferry runs until 15s, then returns to central arena. Ferry is an AnimatableBody3D, not a decorative floating object.
- Five shuffled crystal types: +25% damage for 5s, +25% movement for 5s, 10% max HP heal, immediately reset special cooldown, +40% knockback for 5s. First at 10s, next every 8s until 45s; only one on the map at a time. Both fighters may collect them.
- Six destructible cover objects: four core pillars/walls, two satellite pillars. Hit with normal melee or special/area/projectile attacks. Debris has no collision.
- From 30s storm transition (sky, fog, sun, wind/cloud movement); lightning telegraphs from 35s every 4s, orange 1.8m ring for 1.25s, 8% max HP hazard damage. Both fighters can be hit. The player dodge window still avoids the hit.
- Camera shake and HUD warnings. AI uses centerline escape waypoints, sprint, obstacle steering, gap jumps and floor probes; avoids lightning and does not deliberately pursue players onto islands already in warning. This is heuristic, not navmesh/pathfinding; no guarantee it survives every knockback or fall.
- Existing 45s sudden death, fighter stats, rig clips, attack animations, projectiles and core game remain.
