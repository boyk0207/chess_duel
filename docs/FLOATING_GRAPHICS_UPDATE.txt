VISUAL CHANGES
- Camera-local sky, ambient fill, warm directional sunlight and fog.
- Moving low-poly clouds, eight distant islands, scrolling waterfall shaders.
- Visible floors matching the collision boxes, cliff layers, visible plank decks.
- Grass shader, decorative vegetation with gentle sway, small rocks and flowers.
- Decorations are children of the relevant collapsing parts and have no collision.
- New cyan jump-pad rings and faceted golden pickup.
- Hide chess scenery during 3D duels; restore when returning to the board.
- Compatibility renderer selected for eventual Web export.

No changes to rig assets, damage, cooldowns, collision shape dimensions or collapse schedule.
Visual effects use basic materials, low-poly meshes and spatial shaders, not Forward+-only GI.

RELEASE EXPORT
No export preset was present in the source project. When creating the itch.io Web export,
set Resources > export selected scenes (scenes/main.tscn and dependencies) or exclude the test
files scenes/tests/rig_preview.tscn, scripts/tests/rig_preview.gd, scenes/tests/animation_lab.tscn, scripts/tests/animation_lab.gd and tools/*; or exclude scenes/tests/*, scripts/tests/*, docs/* and tools/*.
Do not exclude scripts/rig_factory.gd, rig GLBs, or the new graphics resources.
This development ZIP intentionally retains the tests.
